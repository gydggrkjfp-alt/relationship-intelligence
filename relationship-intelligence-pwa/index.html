How to use this app

Start with a snapshot. Capture one real interaction while it is fresh. Over time, snapshots build the card’s trajectory, repair patterns, attachment loops, and ecosystem impact.

  1. Choose or create a card for the person, pet, child, boss, friend, partner, or family member.
  2. Write what happened using facts first, then separate the story you are telling yourself.
  3. Move the sliders based on this interaction only, not your entire history with the person.
  4. Save the snapshot. Later you can open Card Detail for deeper sliders, graphs, and strategy modes.

Goal: avoid one-time emotional verdicts. The app is designed to detect patterns across repeated interactions.

Slider help: tap a ? button to learn what each category means.
